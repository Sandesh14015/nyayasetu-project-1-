

📌 Project Description

NyayaSetu AI is an AI-powered chatbot and interactive dashboard developed for the Department of Justice to enhance accessibility, transparency, and efficiency in judicial services. The system acts as a virtual legal assistant that enables users to easily access information related to court procedures, case status, legal aid, and various eServices such as eFiling and ePay.

The platform integrates multiple judicial resources into a single unified interface, allowing users to interact through natural language queries. It provides real-time assistance for services like checking case status, understanding legal procedures, accessing National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) insights, and locating nearby courts or legal aid centers.

Unlike traditional chatbots, NyayaSetu AI goes beyond static responses by offering guided workflows, intelligent suggestions, and simplified explanations of complex legal processes. The system is designed to support multilingual interaction, voice input, and continuous learning through user feedback, ensuring improved performance over time.

By bridging the gap between citizens and the judicial system, NyayaSetu AI promotes access to justice, especially for individuals with limited legal knowledge or resources. The solution is scalable, user-friendly, and aligned with ongoing digital initiatives such as the eCourts Project and Tele-Law services.

🎯 Objectives
To provide a single access point for judicial services
To simplify complex legal procedures using AI
To enable users to check case status and court data easily
To assist citizens in accessing legal aid and government services
To improve user experience through interactive and guided assistance
